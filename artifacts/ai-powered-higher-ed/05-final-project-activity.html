Final Project Activity

AI-Powered Higher Education

Polishing your student experience blueprint

In this activity, you'll use guiding questions and an AI prompt to polish the final draft of your blueprint.

AI-Driven Student Experience Blueprint

You'll complete all sections of your blueprint and refine your drafting by collaborating with your AI conversation partner and by self-assessing your work. 

 

Instructions

Follow the steps below to complete this activity.

Section 1: Problem Context
  • Is the problem clearly stated and specific to your professional context?
  • Who is most affected by this challenge? Have you named those groups clearly?
  • What data or experience supports your understanding of the problem?
  • Have you avoided vague language like “students struggle” and instead shown how or why they struggle?
  • Does the problem connect to a real opportunity to improve equity, belonging, or success?
Section 2: Proposed AI-Driven Teaching/Engagement Strategy
  • Is the solution a logical response to the problem you’ve described?
  • Have you clearly identified where and how AI is being used in the design?
  • Is the AI use appropriate for your role and context (e.g., content generation, feedback support, analysis)?
  • Is your strategy inclusive? Does it account for diverse learning needs, modalities, or student experiences?
  • Could this solution be realistically implemented in your professional setting?
Section 3: Assessment or Evaluation Plan
  • Have you defined what success looks like—for you and for students?
  • Are your indicators measurable, meaningful, and aligned to your strategy?
  • Are you using the right kind of data for your role (e.g., assessments vs. event feedback)?
  • How are you ensuring that your assessment or evaluation approach is fair, ethical, and inclusive?
  • If AI is used to generate assessments or interpret results, how will you review and validate its output?
Section 4: Feedback and Iteration Plan
  • Have you identified how you’ll gather real student feedback on your solution (e.g., surveys, informal check-ins, peer conversations)?
  • How will AI support you in analyzing that feedback? And how will you stay in the loop to ensure critical voices aren’t overlooked?
  • What’s your plan for updating or adjusting your strategy based on feedback?
  • How will you know which changes are urgent or high-impact?
  • Have you considered ethical transparency—how you’ll share with students that their feedback shaped change?

Once you have completed all the steps, bring your draft to your AI collaborator and ask for feedback using the prompt below.

PROMPT

I’ve drafted a project to address a real student learning or experience challenge in higher education. It includes four parts: the problem context, an AI-supported teaching or engagement strategy, an assessment or evaluation plan, and a feedback and iteration plan. I’d like your help reviewing my full project to identify areas that could be improved, clarified, or made more student-centered.

Please start by evaluating whether the problem I’ve identified is specific, grounded in real experience or data, and clearly connected to student needs. Let me know if the context feels too vague or if I should better explain who is affected and why it matters.

Next, take a look at my proposed AI-supported strategy. Does it align with the problem I described? Have I clearly explained how AI is being used to support the solution? I’d also appreciate feedback on whether the strategy seems inclusive of different learning needs, practical for implementation in a higher ed setting, and appropriate for my role.

For the assessment or evaluation plan, please tell me whether the success indicators I’ve chosen are meaningful and measurable. Are my data sources appropriate, and have I described how AI might help with evaluation without replacing human review? Let me know if I’ve adequately considered ethical and inclusive practices in my approach.

Finally, review my feedback and iteration plan. Have I described a realistic and student-centered way to collect and respond to feedback on my solution? Do I explain how AI will assist without doing all the interpretive work? Let me know whether the iteration plan feels aligned to real student needs and includes a way to close the loop with learners so they know their voices are being heard.

Please review my responses holistically and let me know where I could be more specific, inclusive, or aligned with equitable practice. If any sections feel vague, disconnected, or misaligned with the goals, I’d love suggestions for how to improve them.

Criteria for success

Use the criteria for success below to self-assess your draft so far!

  • Problem context: Your blueprint for a strong problem context includes a specific challenge grounded in your actual work, evidence from experience or data supporting your understanding and a clear connection to improving student experiences.
  • AI-driven strategy: You've included a direct response to your defined problem, a clear explanation of how and where AI is being used and a realistic plan that could be implemented in your setting.
  • Assessment or evaluation plan: You've clearly defined what success looks like, identified measurable success metrics or data and addressed any ethical concerns about your solution.
  • Iteration plan: Your blueprint contains specific method for gathering feeback from students and an actionable plan for iterating on your solution in the future.  
  • Clarity of thought and drafting effort: Your work shows you're applying the learning from the course to your project.

AI-Powered Higher Education

Course Overview

What you will learn in this course

Welcome to AI-Powered Higher Education—a course designed for faculty, student support staff and academic leaders who are ready to explore how generative AI can meaningfully impact teaching, learning and the student experience. You’ll explore the potential of AI to support better decisions, improve student engagement and close feedback loops while also considering its limitations and risks.

Whether you're using AI to analyze learning data, enhance your instruction, design equitable assessments or improve student services, this course will guide you through thoughtful strategies you can apply in your everyday work.

 

What will I do in this course?

  • Explore AI tools and techniques that support learning and student success.
  • Apply ethical frameworks to assess and guide AI use in higher education.
  • Develop an AI-driven strategy, where you’ll pinpoint a real challenge in your context and design a solution for it.

 

What am I going to walk away with?

  • A ready-to-use AI blueprint tailored to a real student experience challenge in your professional context—whether it’s instructional, advisory or administrative.
  • Hands-on experience using generative AI tools to analyze student data, generate teaching and support materials and close feedback loops.
  • A flexible evaluation plan for measuring the impact of your AI-supported strategies using real-world indicators that matter in higher education.
  • Ethical and inclusive design practices you can apply to any AI-integrated workflow, with special attention to bias, accessibility and transparency.
  • A final project portfolio piece that demonstrates your critical thinking, applied skills and vision for using AI to improve the student experience.

 

Learning objectives

  • Critically evaluate the capabilities and limitations of generative AI in the context of higher education.
  • Analyze student learning and experience data using AI tools to identify patterns, gaps and opportunities for intervention.
  • Design AI-supported strategies for teaching, engagement, and assessment that are ethical, inclusive and pedagogically sound.
  • Apply AI to student feedback loops to continuously improve programs, services and instruction.
  • Develop and communicate an AI-informed solution to a real-world student experience challenge in higher education.

Project description: AI-driven Student Experience Blueprint

AI-driven Student Experience Blueprint

Throughout this course, you will develop a strategy that uses AI to address a student experience gap. The goal is to use AI both as a collaborator to design the solution and as a part of the solution itself. In building this strategy, you're preparing to use AI reponsibly and strategically in your work. 

 

The challenge

Build an AI-driven strategy for addressing a student learning or experience issue in a higher education context. Along the way, leverage AI as a collaborator to analyze data, and design a AI-supported intervention that include opportunities for students to give feedback you can use to iterate. 

Deliverables

For this course, you'll complete a proposal template designed to guide you through the process of brainstorming, developling, planning and justifying an AI-driven higher education strategy.

Your proposal will include the following:

  • Defined problem & context
  • Data-informed analysis using AI
  • Proposed AI-supported intervention
  • Plan for feedback, measurement and iteration

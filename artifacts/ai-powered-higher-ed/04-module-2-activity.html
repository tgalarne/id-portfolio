Module 2 Activity

AI-Powered Higher Education

Designing your intervention

In this activity, you'll begin designing the solution or intervention you'll use to address the problem you identified in Module 1. Using the learning from this module, you'll consider the use-cases for AI that would best fit your context and the challenge you chose to focus on. 

AI-Driven Student Experience Blueprint

You will begin drafting the Proposed AI-Driven Teaching/Engagement Strategy section of your template. This activity is the starting point for designing your AI-driven solution. 

 

Instructions

Follow the steps below to complete this activity.

Step 1: Revisit your problem

Look back at your draft problem statement from Module 1 and take stock before thinking about what your solution might look like. 

  • Who is impacted?
  • What kind of change or support is needed?
  • What would success look like?
Step 2: Brainstorm the type of intervention you’ll need

Take a moment to list out the various solutions you might consider to address the problem. 

Examples of solutions could be:

  • A revised lesson plan
  • An advising or outreach script
  • A workshop or event outline
  • A learning resource or tool
  • A student communication strategy
Step 3: Co-create with AI

Revisit your discussion with your AI collaborator and brainstorm together about some of your ideas. 

Prompt:

    • "I’m designing an AI-supported solution for [insert problem and context here]. I’m thinking about the following solutions: [list your solutions]. The goal is to [insert goal here]. Can you help me decide what avenue to take?" 

You can also include:

  • Audience (e.g., “first-year students,” “neurodivergent learners”)
  • Modality (e.g., “for asynchronous online learners”)

Iterate: Refine your prompt, ask for variations, or request edits.

Step 4: Evaluate and adapt

Critically review your AI-generated output.

Ask yourself:

  • Is this content inclusive and contextually appropriate?
  • Would this resonate with the students I serve?
  • What needs revision, personalization, or clarification?
  • Is anything missing that I would normally include?

Make edits or notes in your template as needed.

Step 5: Document your solution draft

Write up a short summary of your AI-supported solution.

Include:

  • A title or name for your idea
  • The type of solution you’re creating
  • Your refined AI-generated content (or excerpts)
  • Notes on what you’d adapt further and why

Once you have completed all the steps, bring your draft to your AI collaborator and ask for feedback using the prompt below.

PROMPT

“I've started drafting an AI-supported solution to address a student experience challenge. The goal of the solution is [insert goal]. I’m targeting [audience] in [modality]. Can you help me evaluate whether the content feels inclusive, practical and aligned with the challenge I’m trying to address? Is AI integrating meaningfully into the solution or is there room for improvement. ”

Criteria for success

Use the criteria for success below to self-assess your draft so far!

  • Connection between the problem and possible solutions: Your solution(s), even if drafty, is clearly connected and relevant to the student experience challenge. 
  • Intentional use of AI in the solution: You've made a good-faith effort to integrate an AI tool into your solution, with attention to ethics and suitability (i.e., not for the sake of using AI but because it justifiably supports your solution)
  • Clarity of thought and drafting effort: Your drafting, even if it's incomplete or rough, shows you're applying the learning from this module to your solution.

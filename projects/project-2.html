Professional Skills: Emotional Intelligence

Excerpts and artifacts from a course built in Rise for ASU's CPL portfolio, with versions hosted on both Canvas and Coursera.

Audience
Individuals seeking to enhance leadership and interpersonal skills, support diverse teams, or better navigate emotionally complex work environments.
Tools
  • Articulate Rise
  • Articulate Storyline
  • AWS S3

Additional context

The Professional Skills series represents a core direct-to-learner product within ASU Learning Enterprise's CPL Portfolio, complementing its job-, sector-, and industry-specific learning offerings. Alongside colleagues on the CPL Learning Design Team, I originally designed and developed roughly half of the 28 courses before taking over ongoing management, administration, and cross-platform expansion of the full product line.

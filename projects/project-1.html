AI-Powered Higher Education

Excerpts and artifacts from a Canvas-built and -hosted course made for ASU's Career and Professional Learning portfolio.

Audience
Higher education faculty and staff seeking to integrate AI into learning design and delivery, student support, or pedagogical planning.
Tools
  • Canvas
  • HTML
  • AWS S3
  • ChatGPT
  • Claude

Additional context

ASU Learning Enterprise's Career and Professional Learning portfolio lacked foundational learning content on the topic of generative AI. Alongside colleagues on the CPL Learning Design Team, I designed and developed a four-course series for early- and mid-career learners covering the Foundations of Generative AI and its applications in Higher Education, K-12 Education, Learning Analytics, and Workplace Transformation to help fill this gap.

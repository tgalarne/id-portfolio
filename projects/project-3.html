Reknowable Resources

A public-facing collection of Sustainability-oriented micro-learning experiences including short-form videos, podcasts, carousels, and games.

Audience
Individuals in Aramark-operated locations across collegiate hospitality, corporate, and healthcare lines of business interested in learning more about organizational efforts and personal potential to improve everyday sustainability.
Tools
  • WordPress
  • Audacity
  • Genially
  • Google Analytics

Additional context

Reknowable Resources is a collection of mobile-first microlearning experiences intended for access primarily via strategically deployed QR codes in Aramark-operated hospitality locations. I led information architecture and communication design across formats, drafted podcast scripts, storyboarded videos, assisted with multimedia production, and managed ongoing administration and engagement tracking.
